\textbf{Claim 1.}
\textit{Attention logit growth appears in small Transformers at high learning
rates, and qk-layernorm resolves it at both small and large scale, measurably
reducing LR sensitivity.}
We verify this by training models with and without qk-layernorm across several
learning rates, tracking the max attention logit over training, and computing
LR sensitivity for each configuration (Section~3.1.1 and Figures~1--2
of~\cite{wortsman2023smallscale}).


\textbf{Claim 2.}
\textit{Output logit divergence occurs in Transformer models when weight decay is absent, and z-loss prevents this instability.}

We evaluate this claim by training models under the four configurations
$\{$z-loss, no z-loss$\} \times \{$weight decay, no weight decay$\}$
and tracking the mean and variance of the output logits throughout training.

Contrary to the findings of~\cite{wortsman2023smallscale}, we do not observe clear logit divergence in the absence of weight decay.
