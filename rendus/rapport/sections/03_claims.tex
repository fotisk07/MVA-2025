\section{Claims Under Verification}
\label{sec:claims}

\wip{This is not yet definite, to be changed when experiments are done}
\textbf{Claim 1.}
\textit{Attention logit growth appears in small Transformers at high learning
rates, and qk-layernorm resolves it at both small and large scale, measurably
reducing LR sensitivity.}
We verify this by training models with and without qk-layernorm across several
learning rates, tracking the max attention logit over training, and computing
LR sensitivity for each configuration (Section~3.1.1 and Figures~1--2
of~\cite{wortsman2023smallscale}).

\textbf{Claim 2.}
\textit{Output logit divergence occurs in models of any size when weight decay
is absent, and z-loss reliably prevents it.}
We verify this by training the four combinations of $\{$z-loss, no z-loss$\}
\times \{$weight decay, no weight decay$\}$ and monitoring the output logit
mean throughout training (Section~3.1.2 and Figures~3--4
of~\cite{wortsman2023smallscale}).
